    Step 1 of 3: get a free key

    Get a free API key

    Phrasor sends your draft to an AI model to rewrite it, so it needs a key from a provider. Groq and Google Gemini both have a free plan, and neither asks for a card.

    Groq (suggested)Fast, and the model Phrasor is tuned for.
    Google GeminiAlso free. Choose it if Groq is not available to you.

    The links open in your browser. Copy the key, then come back here.

    Paste and test your key

    The key is stored encrypted for your Windows account and is never shown again. Using another provider? Skip this and use Settings, Model.

    API keys

    Stored encrypted for your Windows account. A saved key is never shown again, here or anywhere else.

    Shortcuts

    Click a shortcut, then press the keys you want. Include Ctrl or Alt. Esc cancels, Backspace clears. Your shortcuts work again once you save, discard or leave.

    Voice

    Windows also has free built-in voice typing (Win+H) if you would rather not send audio to a cloud service.

    Requirements

    Text you write once and Phrasor adds, word for word, to the end of each rewrite of that kind. "All tasks" is added to every rewrite. It is never sent to the model, and a rewrite that comes back unchanged gets nothing added.

    Data

    What Phrasor storesSettings: %APPDATA%\Phrasor\settings.json. API keys: secrets.dat in the same folder, encrypted for your Windows account.Logs: events, timings and error types, kept for 7 days. Never your text or keys.History: only what you press Save on, in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Phrasor\History\history.dat, encrypted for your Windows account (up to 500 items, each side cut at 20,000 characters).
    What leaves your PCText you choose to rewrite (with a shortcut, or in Try it) goes to the model provider you set up. Dictated audio goes to the voice provider. Free tiers may use data to improve their services (Google says so for Gemini's free tier). The update check contacts GitHub.
